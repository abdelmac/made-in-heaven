A final information notice must state controller/contact details, purposes and legal bases, recipients/transfers, retention periods **or meaningful criteria**, applicable rights and complaint/withdrawal routes, and whether requested data is mandatory. See [CNIL transparency guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/conformite-rgpd-information-des-personnes-et-transparence) and [GDPR Article 13](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/reglement-europeen-protection-donnees/chapitre3). Keep the text understandable for the actual audience.

Professional publisher notices and the LCEN exception for genuinely nonprofessional publishers are separate questions. Free local access and a nickname do not establish that exception. A GDPR draft does not automatically require publishing a home address and phone, but French publisher rules and store identity/trader rules can require further details. Assess the actual paid activity before publication rather than filling gaps with invented registration or contact information. See [LCEN Article 1-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000049568614), [French professional website notices](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37351?lang=fr), [Apple enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) and [Google developer identity](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en).
